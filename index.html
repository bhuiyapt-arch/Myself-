About our academy

Live Quran learning, built around each student.

Al-Hikmah Online Quran Academy teaches the Quran live and one-on-one to children and adults across Australia. Every lesson is built around the student’s level, and every family receives a clear progress update each month.

Our tutors are patient, experienced and friendly, so students feel confident from the very first class.
